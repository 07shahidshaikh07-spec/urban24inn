6. **Gold on maroon fails WCAG as text** (2.3–3.2:1 with the sampled colours). Gold can only be ornament or large display type. Body text on maroon must be cream.

## 4. What guests actually say

I read about 60 Dalchini reviews: Google's "most relevant" sort plus everything from the last 12 months. Google's own topic counts are in brackets.

**Praised**
- **Mutton thali** [17]. Reviewers describe mutton rassa, dry mutton, keema and egg masala, served with pandhra rassa, solkadi and soft chapati.
- **Pandhra rassa** [6] and **solkadi** [7] are the most consistently loved items. One reviewer ranked the rassa above versions they had eaten in Kolhapur.
- **Chicken thalis**, including the Dalchini Special Chicken Thali. It comes with *alni* in place of keema. A *gavran* (desi) chicken thali is also mentioned.
- Also named: chicken ukkad, kharda fry, gavran chicken fry, angara kebab, chicken crispy, tandoori chicken, manchow soup, masala papad, veg maratha, veg kofta, paneer pahadi and masala bhindi.
- **Parking:** "one of the few places" in Latur with proper parking.
- **Family setting:** veg and non-veg at the same table, quiet enough to talk, groups of 3–8.

**Criticised**
- **Service speed** is the most repeated complaint (2023–2026): late order-taking, slow serving, missing items. Waits of 10–30 minutes, one over an hour.
- **Value and consistency, recently.** A Nov 2026 review says the ₹525 mutton thali came with only two pieces in the curry and small portions, and that quality and quantity have dropped since earlier visits (it also calls the staff polite and attentive). Other reviews call prices high for the taste.
- **Biryani** is called average. **Vegetarians are split:** some recommend it, others warn veg diners off.
- A banquet-booking complaint (Nov 2026), and several recent 1★ ratings with no text.
- "Always closed when I visit" (about a year ago). Wrong hours online cost covers.

**Integrity flag.** Two 5★ reviews from about 7 months ago share near-identical wording. Two from about a month ago list the same long string of dishes. **We should not quote any of these.**

**What the site should lean on**
1. **The thali, explained.** Say exactly what comes on each plate. This sets expectations and answers the "fewer pieces" complaint honestly.
2. **Rassa and solkadi** as the house signatures.
3. **A veg menu with equal standing**, clearly marked. This answers the split veg verdict.
4. **Correct hours, parking, and one-tap Call/WhatsApp.**
5. **No superlatives.** "Best in Latur" next to a 3.6 rating backfires. Let named dishes do the work.

The website can't fix service speed. The owner should know it is the top complaint.

**Reviews on the site (your call, Q3).** Option A: the live Google rating with a link, plus 2–3 short, dish-specific excerpts from established reviewers (name, month, link). Option B: rating and link only. For the hotel, 5 ratings and 3 OTA reviews are too few to quote, so it gets a link only. Excerpt candidates (each rates the food 5/5 and talks about the thalis): Vikash Sapkota (about Aug 2026), Avadhesh Pal (Local Guide, about 2024), Paulomi Patel (Local Guide, about 2024). I'd re-check each one's overall star rating and exact wording before quoting.

**Contrast checks (WCAG AA needs 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text)**
- Gold `#D19B60` on maroon: **2.30:1** (sunlit) / **3.23:1** (shade). Fails for text. Use it only for ornament or large display on a deepened maroon.
- Cream `#F2E8D3` on maroon `#7E3E40`: **6.51:1**, passes. On sunlit maroon `#A8494F` it is 4.63:1, which barely passes.
- Charcoal `#433F42` on cream: 8.51:1, passes. Gold on charcoal: 4.22:1, which fails for small text.

**Motifs to carry forward**
- The hand-lettered **दालचिनी** with its leaf sprig.
- The **3D triangle tiles** as the hotel's geometric texture.
- The glass wall banded by maroon fascias, as layered horizontal bands.
- **Timber, rattan pendants, hanging creepers and the jaali counter** for Dalchini's warmth.
- The **green and red veg/non-veg marks already on the sign**, beside the logo, which link straight to the menu's marks.
